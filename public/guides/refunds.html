Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: [TODO: YYYY-MM-DD]

Cancel anytime

Manage or cancel your subscription at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders using the email you paid with. After cancelling, your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then the account moves to the Free plan. Your API key and stored templates are kept; templates above the Free plan's limit stay stored but you cannot add new ones.

14-day refund on your first payment

If DocForge is not right for you, email within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, no questions asked. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

Renewals and upgrades

Renewal payments are not refundable, but you can cancel before the next renewal. If something went wrong on our side (for example an outage that stopped you rendering), write to us and we will make it right.

Exceptions

Accounts suspended for breaking the acceptable use rules are not refunded.
